# Temporary portrait assets

These fictional portraits were made with the built-in image-generation tool for
the user's requested Team and Testimonials templates. They do not depict verified
employees or customers. Every seeded card is labelled **Sample template**.

Saved assets:

- `public/assets/sample-portraits/portrait-01.png`
- `public/assets/sample-portraits/portrait-02.png`
- `public/assets/sample-portraits/portrait-03.png`

The original generated files are preserved. The site uses each image for one team
sample and one testimonial sample. Replace the photographs, names and all sample
text with approved content before final launch. No endorsement or staff identity
is attributed to these fictional subjects.
